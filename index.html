Services

Five areas of work, start to finish

The same five clauses used in every OSSCS engagement proposal — establishment through to audit.

I

Establishment & signage / marking

Establishment of new and old factories to compliance and audit norms, including the policies and procedures the Factory Act and other government norms require. Covers all industry signage work, including yellow and zebra floor marking.

II

Returns — monthly, half-yearly, annual

Preparation and filing of monthly PF & ESI challans and payroll, plus every return submission on the government's required schedule.

III

Licence work

End-to-end licensing for new and old factories — map approval, factory licence, RSPCB clearances (CTE, CTO, authorisation, hazardous waste) and Fire NOC — handled within timeframe at minimum cost.
